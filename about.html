Rooted in Riverbend Since 2016

Riverbend Yoga Studio started in a converted garage with six mats and one teacher. A decade later, we're still a small, independent studio — we've just grown into a full team of instructors who share the same belief: yoga should meet you exactly where you are.

Maria Torres

Maria founded Riverbend Yoga Studio after a decade of teaching in larger, faster-paced studios left her wanting something more personal. She leads our Gentle Flow and Vinyasa Basics classes, and specializes in helping first-time students feel steady on the mat.

Jordan Lee

Jordan trained in restorative and trauma-informed yoga and leads our Tuesday and Thursday evening Restorative sessions. Their classes are slow, quiet, and prop-heavy — a chance to unwind after a demanding day.

Priya Anand

Priya teaches our Chair Yoga class and brings a background in physical therapy to every session. She works closely with students managing limited mobility, chronic pain, or recovery from injury or surgery.

Our Approach

Every instructor at Riverbend offers modifications for every pose, because we believe the best class is the one that works for the body that showed up that day. There's no dress code, no experience requirement, and no pressure to keep up with anyone else in the room.
