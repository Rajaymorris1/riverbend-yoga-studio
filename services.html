Classes & Services

Every class at Riverbend is offered at a few different levels of intensity, so you can choose what fits your body that day. Browse our regular classes below, or scroll down for the full weekly schedule and pricing.

Our Classes

Beginner friendly

Gentle Flow

Slow, supported movement paired with breathwork. The best starting point if you're new to yoga or returning after time away.

All levels

Restorative Evenings

Long-held, props-supported poses designed to calm the nervous system after a long day. Bolsters and blankets provided.

Intermediate

Vinyasa Basics

A steady, breath-linked flow that builds strength and stamina while still leaving room for modifications.

Accessible seated

Chair Yoga

A seated and standing practice that supports limited mobility, balance concerns, or recovery from injury.
