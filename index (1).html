Find Your Calm, Right in Riverbend

Riverbend Yoga Studio exists to make yoga feel welcoming and approachable for everyone in our community, no matter their age, background, or experience level. We believe movement and breath are for every body.

New to Yoga? You're in the Right Place

Every class at Riverbend is beginner friendly. Our instructors offer modifications for every pose, and there is never any pressure to keep up with anyone else in the room. Come as you are.

This Week's Featured Class: Gentle Flow

Our Gentle Flow class focuses on slow, supported movement, ideal for beginners and those returning to their practice.
